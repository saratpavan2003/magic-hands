🐍 Snakes & Ladders

A pop-up book game that appears in your room!

👥 Who's playing?

    🔒 The camera picture stays on your device. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. Ask a grown-up before you tap “Allow”.

    📖 How it works

    📚

    Find the book

    A bookcase appears in your room. Look around with your phone, then tap the glowing green book.

    ✨

    Open it

    The book flies to you and opens. The board pops up, with snakes and ladders rising out of it.

    🎲

    Roll!

    Tap Roll or shake the phone. Climb ladders, slide down snakes, and land exactly on 100 to win. A 6 rolls again!

    💡 Tips

    • Point your phone at a table or the floor, so the book looks like it's sitting there.
    • Drag sideways to turn the book around. Pinch to make it bigger.
    • Lost the bookcase? Tap 🎯 to bring it back in front of you.
